Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Dr-Crown (dr-crown.com) is a dental laboratory case-tracking platform operated from the Sultanate of Oman by Paradise Harbours (paradiseharbours.com). This policy explains what data the platform handles and why, in line with Oman's Personal Data Protection Law (Royal Decree 6/2022).

What we collect

  • Account data — your email address, name, phone number, and the clinic or laboratory details you register (name, location, contact details).
  • Case data entered by clinics — patient name and optionally a patient ID and WhatsApp number, prescription details (restoration type, materials, shades, tooth chart), clinical and shade photographs, and case notes exchanged between clinic and laboratory.
  • Billing data — invoice numbers, amounts, and payment status between laboratories and clinics.
  • Technical data — device and browser information, sign-in records, and error reports used to keep the service secure and working.

Why we process it

Solely to provide the service: routing prescriptions from clinics to their chosen laboratories, tracking case progress, sending service emails (case notifications, staff invitations, payment reminders, security alerts), and producing analytics for the clinic or laboratory that owns the data. We do not sell data or use it for advertising.

Patient data — the clinic's role

Patient information is entered by the treating clinic, which remains responsible for it — including obtaining any consent required from the patient before entering their details and photographs. Dr-Crown processes this data only on the clinic's instructions and makes it visible only to that clinic and the laboratory it sends the case to.

Where data is stored

Data is stored on secure managed cloud infrastructure (Supabase) and transactional email is delivered via Resend. This means data may be processed on servers located outside the Sultanate of Oman, protected by encryption in transit and access controls. Access within the platform is enforced per organisation — a clinic sees only its own cases, a laboratory only cases sent to it.

Retention

Case and account data is retained while the associated clinic or laboratory account remains active, so that clinical and billing history stays available to its owners. Accounts and their organisation's data can be deleted on request.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and clinics may do the same on behalf of their patients. Contact us at info@paradiseharbours.com and we will respond as required by the Personal Data Protection Law.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the "last updated" date above changes and significant updates will be announced in the app.
